# Ars Grammatica: audit of the exercises and answers, Phase 2 (29 Oct 2026)

Scope: every exercise set, section review, and question point of the course: the 211 exercise sets in data/exercises/ (7,160 items), the question points of the 226 decks that have them (3,108 variants) in data/decks/, and the four generated sets of data/latin-forms.js, each run 400 times at each of eight sizes (32,000 items per set). Every item was read with its options, key, explanation, accepted typed answers, and hints; every answer was worked independently. The data/languages/*.js files hold no questions. The recitation outlines (61 decks) are statements, not questions, and were audited in Phase 1. No course file was changed.

Each finding gives the set or deck and the item, the current text, the problem, the category (1 correct answer and fact, 2 answerability, 3 distractors, 4 explanations, 5 typed answers, 6 language, forms, and translations, 7 style, 8 coverage and levels, 9 mechanics), the severity (error, should fix, suggestion; a wrong key or a wrong statement of fact counts as an error, and "minor" in the body means suggestion), whether the change is **mechanical** (safe to apply without you) or **needs Timothy**, and the proposed wording after the arrow. The explanations' "Correct." and "Incorrect." are added by the course, so they are not checked in the data.

## 1. Summary

Findings: **239** in all: **38 errors**, 57 should fix, 144 suggestions. Of these, 188 are mechanical and 51 need you. Several are class findings that cover many items (for example, 116 explanations that end in a stray gloss, 136 Greek explanations that capitalize a mentioned Greek form, 89 roman *passé simple* / *passé composé*, 501 Section IX prompts that end in an ellipsis); each counts once here.

The keys are sound almost everywhere. The errors are of four kinds: a key or an explanation that contradicts the course's own card or another item (φεῦ, the possessive "adjective", who/whom, the Section I review ids); an item with two defensible answers (Hercules and the Swan of Avon, *Marta la escribió*, *patrie*, Darío's *sonoro*); a slip in the data (*die Garten*, "by his uncle", *Σ* for the final sigma, *mē* in English); and a statement of fact stronger than the source (Quintilian's order, the participles of Catiline). The largest group needing you is translation: several language sets give English renderings of prose that are not recorded in sources/TRANSLATIONS-USED.md, and some differ from the translators recorded for the same passages.

## 3. Mechanics

- Typed answers in Spanish, French, and German (es 96, fr 64, de 104 items): every character of every accepted answer is on an English keyboard or on the course's letter keys for that language; none is missing. Spanish and French keep accents in grading, so an unaccented answer is marked wrong, as the instructions say. German answers typed with *ae*, *oe*, *ue*, and *ss* are accepted (course.js gradeTypedAnswer), as the German instructions promise. Latin and Greek typed items were checked for macrons earlier in this audit: no problem.
- Duplicate item ids: the 45 items of iii.review-indeclinable reuse the ids of i.review (error above; practice.js keys the review boxes by id). The Spanish parts sets only seem to share ids: their ids carry the set name.
- Section reviews: every review's items point to existing decks with a line in data/tiers.js, and every deck of each section is covered; Section III's two reviews together cover all its decks.
- Two-option items: 15 (the declinable/indeclinable items of the French and German parts sets, and v.ex.period pe-L3-02, -06, -07); the first twelve are two-way by nature.
- Correct option the longest in more than a third of a set: only v.ex.clause (7 of 16) and the question points of i.ambiguity, i.consignification, i.imposition, i.sign, i.syncategorematic, iii.reasons, and prelim.criteria; no language set.
- Pools: one set has fewer than 20 items (x.ex.imitation, 10 long passages). Seventeen general pools have fewer than 40 items: iii.ex.figure 30, iii.ex.noun-1 39, iii.ex.verb-1 37, iv.review 30, ix.review 30, v.ex.clause 32, v.ex.congruity 24, v.ex.figures 24, v.ex.mood 32, v.ex.order 24, v.ex.phrase 31, vi.ex.barbarism 24, vi.ex.metaplasm 24, vi.ex.solecism 24, vi.ex.usage 24, viii.review 30, x.ex.imitation 10. notes/GUIDED-POOLS-UNDER-40.md (23 Oct) is out of date: iii.ex.noun-2, iii.ex.participles, iii.ex.pronouns, iii.ex.verb-2, v.ex.agreement, v.ex.dependence, v.ex.government, and v.ex.period now have 40 or more. In the language layer 129 sets have fewer than 40 items (most have 20 to 24).
- Empty explanations: lang.iii-decl.grc/fill-ex, 40 items (finding in the Greek layer).
