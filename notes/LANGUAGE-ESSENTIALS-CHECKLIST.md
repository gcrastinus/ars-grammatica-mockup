# The essentials of grammar, language by language: checklist

Written 20 Oct 2026 before the build (Timothy's governing aim: each language track should amount, more or less, to an "essentials of grammar" for that language). The topics are those of a short standard essentials grammar (Allen and Greenough's *New Latin Grammar* in outline; Smyth's *Greek Grammar for Colleges*, Parts I–IV in outline; a Duden or Hammer school grammar for German; a Grevisse or Larousse school grammar for French; a standard school grammar for English). Each topic is marked **covered** (with the study deck and its exercise), **partial**, or **missing**, at the start of the pass (state "before"). The final state is in the section "After the pass" at the end, and in notes/LANGUAGES-FINISH-REPORT.md.

Ids are the screen item ids (`lang.<bar>.<code>/<slug>`). "ex" is the exercise paired with the deck.
