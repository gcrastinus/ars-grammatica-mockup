Ars Grammatica

The first art of the trivium
Grammar is the art of speaking and writing correctly. The course follows the order of the art: the definition and parts of grammar, the sign and the word, the matter of speech, the parts of speech, construction, the errors and figures of speech, and the reading and writing of the authors. English and Latin are used throughout, with Greek, German, and French where they show something distinctive.

These sets of exercises were designed by Timothy Kearns, PhD, created and maintained with the assistance of AI, and are extensively revised for clarity and precision.

Recitation Tablet

What is recitation?

One of the oldest and best-proven ways to study is to read a section of a book, or study a topic, in one sitting, then close your notes and write down everything you remember, in as much detail as you can. This is called "recitation," and this tablet lets you do it.

After you finish studying a part of the course, close it and open the Recitation Tablet. Choose the part you just finished from the menu, then write down, in order, everything you can remember about it. This is not easy. But the difficulty is what produces results, especially the moment when you feel you remember nothing more and then keep searching.

When you have finished, click "See Full Recitation." You will see a complete outline of the material with every point stated simply. The outline is a checklist: go through it carefully and mark exactly what you included correctly and what you missed. The app will remind you to recite the same material again after a few days, since each new recall fixes it more firmly.

Write a fuller recitation to see the full one.
