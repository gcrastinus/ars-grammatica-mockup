Ars Grammatica

The first art of the trivium
Grammar is the art of speaking and writing correctly. The course follows the order of the art: the definition and parts of grammar, the sign and the word, the matter of speech, the parts of speech, construction, the errors and figures of speech, and the reading and writing of the authors. English and Latin are used throughout, with Greek, German, and French where they show something distinctive.

These sets of exercises were designed by Timothy Kearns, PhD, created and maintained with the assistance of AI, and are extensively revised for clarity and precision.
