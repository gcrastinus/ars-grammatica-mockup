# Quotation check, 19 October 2026

This is a check, not a card pass. Nothing in data/ was changed; the corrections below are proposals, to be applied to the cards when the files in data/ are free.

Scope: (1) every quotation or locus marked "verify," "to be checked," "still unverified," "unreachable," or the like in sources/TRANSLATIONS-USED.md, and the two flags in data/decks/ii.meter.js (caesura) and data/decks/i.imposition.js (many-languages); (2) the Sanskrit, Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Turkish (and Sumerian) forms in the question placeholders of xi.traditions and xi.modern; (3) a sweep of data/decks/ for author-attributed quotations with no entry in TRANSLATIONS-USED.md.

Access. From this container only GitHub is reachable. Perseus, archive.org, the Latin Library, Gutenberg, Wikisource, CCEL, ctext and New Advent refused the connection. The checks therefore used local copies of public-domain texts (Keil's *Grammatici Latini* text of Donatus; Priscian Books I–XI; Diomedes; Bede; Isidore; the KJV; Gutenberg #100 Shakespeare and other Gutenberg texts via GITenberg; PerseusDL TEI files, including Butler's Quintilian) and web-search snippets.

Result categories: **verified** (read word for word in the source); **verified at second hand** (a search engine matched the exact phrase on a copy of the named source, but the page could not be opened); **corrected** (the right text and locus are given); **not found** (where we looked is stated).
