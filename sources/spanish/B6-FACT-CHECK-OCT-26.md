# Spanish, batch B6: fact check of Section V, first half (26 Oct 2026)

Scope: `lang.v.es/agreement`, `government`, `voice`, with their question points and exercises (`agree-ex`, `more-ex`, `voice-ex`).

Sources: MANUAL is the Academy's *Nueva gramática de la lengua española: Manual* (2010), read as a machine-read text (Internet Archive item RAEManualDeLaNuevaGramaticaDeLaLenguaEspanola). A&G is Allen and Greenough's *New Latin Grammar* (1903), read as a machine-read text (Internet Archive item allengreenoughsn01alle; the OCR has lost the macrons of the printed book). The Academy's website still refuses automated access, and the printed books could not be compared (B14). Excerpts: `rae-manual-2010-excerpts-B6.txt`, `allen-greenough-excerpts-B6.txt`. The paragraph numbers are the Manual's own.

How to read the verdicts. "Verified" means that the statement, or the rule that an example applies, is found in the paragraph named. "Course's example" means that the sentence is the course's own, built on a rule that is verified. "Inference" and "one source" mark claims that go into the B14 row of SPANISH-PLAN.md.

The English remarks that open each card (the agreement of "this" and "these," "she walks" and "they walk," and the like) are plain English grammar and are not sourced.

## `government`

*Leísmo* is described in the Academy's own terms. The card gives the Academy's Spanish wording of its three judgments, in italics and with the paragraph named (*no se considera incorrecto*, *no se recomienda*, *carece de prestigio y se considera incorrecto*), and says in plain English what each means. No judgment is made in the course's own voice, and the card quotes no English translation of the Manual.
