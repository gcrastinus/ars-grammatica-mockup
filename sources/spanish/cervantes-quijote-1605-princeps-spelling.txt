SOURCE FILE: forms of the first edition of Don Quijote (1605) named in lang.ix.es/prose, card "The text and its spelling"
WORK: Miguel de Cervantes, El ingenioso hidalgo Don Quixote de la Mancha (Madrid: por Iuan de la Cuesta, 1605). Author died 1616; work first published 1605.
TEXT READ: the Internet Archive item elingeniosohidal00cerguat (a scan of an exemplar of the 1605 edition, from the Biblioteca Ludwig von Mises, Universidad Francisco Marroquín), leaf 29 = folio 1, chapter 1. The words were read from the page image (enlarged crops). The printed book could not be compared (batch B14).
FORMS READ (first page of chapter 1): chapter heading "Que trata de la condicion, y exercicio del famoso hidalgo don Quixote de la Mancha"; "no ha mucho tiempo que viuia vn hidalgo de los de lança en [astillero], adarga antigua, rozin flaco, y galgo corredor. Vna olla de algo mas vaca que carnero, [salpicon] las mas noches, duelos y quebrãtos los Sabados, lantejas los Viernes, algun palomino de añadidura los Domingos: consumian las tres partes de su hazienda."
NOTES: the printer uses the long s (ſ), which looks like f, in some words (astillero, salpicon, ...), and the course does not quote those words from the 1605 text. "quebrãtos" has a tilde over the a (abbreviation for n). The days of the week are capitalized. The card quotes only: Quixote, viuia vn hidalgo, lança, rozin, quebrãtos, Sabados, Viernes, hazienda.
