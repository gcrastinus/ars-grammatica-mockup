# Spanish, batch B1: fact check of the Phase 1 cards (25 Oct 2026)

Scope: every statement of fact on the five cards of `lang.ii.es/orthography`, their question points and the exercise `lang.ii.es/orthography-ex`; the facts on the cards of `lang.ii.es/syllable` and `lang.ii.es/meter`. The Preliminary overview `lang.prelim.es/overview` was not checked in this batch.

The Academy's own website (rae.es) refused automated access (HTTP 403), so its online *Ortografía* pages and the *Diccionario panhispánico de dudas* could not be read. The texts below were read instead from PDFs and pages that could be fetched. Where a source is secondary, the entry says so.

## The syllable and meter cards

These carry rules of the *Ortografía* and of Spanish prosody. They were read statement by statement and each example was recounted by hand; no error of fact was found. The monosyllable rule on the last syllable card agrees with ORT1999 4.5 and with MORALES. What was **not** done: the meter statements were not checked against a page of Tomás Navarro Tomás, *Métrica española*, which is the deck's named source, because no copy was reached. They are textbook doctrine and the counts below were recomputed:

- *Verde que te quiero verde* is eight syllables; *el camino y nada más* is seven written and counts as eight; *que por mayo era, por mayo* is eight with synalepha in *mayo era*.
- *El dulce lamentar de dos pastores* (Garcilaso, Égloga I) is eleven; *La princesa está triste... ¿qué tendrá la princesa?* is two hemistichs of seven, fourteen in all.
- One correction: the synalepha card divided *se hace camino al andar* as "se ha-ce-ca-mi-no al-an-dar", which put the *l* of *al* in the wrong syllable (the syllables are *seha, ce, ca, mi, noa, lan, dar*). The count of seven was right. The card now lists the seven syllables in order.
- The Garcilaso and Machado texts quoted in the meter deck were not collated with an edition in this batch. The *Romance del prisionero* opening and Lorca's *Verde que te quiero verde* were not collated either.
