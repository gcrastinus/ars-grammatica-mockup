# Spanish, batch B8: fact check of Sections VI and VII (26 Oct 2026)

Scope: `lang.vi.es/errors`, `usage` (with `vices-ex`, `errors-ex`) and `lang.vii.es/figures`, `tropes` (with `figures-ex`, `tropes-ex`), with their question points.

Sources: DPD is the Academy's *Diccionario panhispánico de dudas* (2005), read entry by entry in the copies of the Internet Archive (the Academy's site refuses automated access); the excerpts are in `dpd-2005-excerpts-B8.txt`, with the address of each copy. MANUAL is the Academy's *Nueva gramática de la lengua española: Manual* (2010), read in the machine-read text of the Internet Archive (as in B2 to B7); the paragraph numbers are the Manual's own. Neither could be compared with the printed book (B14). The Donatus definitions are those of `lang.vi.la` and `lang.vi.en`, already verified against Holtz's text in an earlier round.

How to read the verdicts. "Verified" means that the statement, or the rule that an example applies, is found in the entry or paragraph named. "Course's example" means that the sentence is the course's own, built on a rule that is verified. "Inference" and "one source" mark claims that go into the B14 row of `SPANISH-PLAN.md`. The English remarks that open each card (the English errors, "Him saw me," "fall" and "autumn") are plain English usage and are not sourced.
