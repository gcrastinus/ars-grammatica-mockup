## Points that differ from the working notes

- **Maudslay's date.** The working notes and Timothy's brief give 1908. The title page of volume 2 (the volume that contains the passage) is dated MDCCCCX (1910). Volume 1 is of 1908. The Sources note says 1910, and says that the Maudslay is a Hakluyt Society edition.
- **Gillespie's date.** The working notes and the brief give 1911. The Internet Archive's record of the Dutton book says "First printing ... March, 1919", and the Gutenberg text reproduces a Grosset & Dunlap printing (copyright 1919, 1922). The course says 1919. A translation first published in the United States in 1919 is in the public domain there.
- **Vedia's date.** The copy read (item bub_gb_RoxsUv3XrCkC) has 1853 on its title page; the Archive catalogs another copy (historiadorespr00vedigoog) as 1852. The working notes said 1852 and 1858. The Sources note gives 1853, the date of the copy from which the page image was read.
- **Valera, *despues*.** The working notes said that the accent on *después* was unclear. At high resolution the page has none, so the deck prints *despues*.
