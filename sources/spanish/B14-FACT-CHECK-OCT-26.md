A search of an optical reading does not prove that a statement is absent; "no statement found" means that.

Changes made in this batch: the Cervantes prologue in X was moved from Aribau to Rodríguez Marín (text, Sources note, a new card "The text and its spelling," the quotation check, and a new source file). The false statements that the Aribau and Reyes editions "print modern spelling" or are "an edition of this kind" were corrected in the Sources note and the card of IX prose; the Sources note and a closing paragraph of the spelling card of IX poetry now say which editions state a modernization. No other passage was changed.

Candidates found for the editions that are not shown, with what a change would involve:

- **Garcilaso XXIII.** Garcilaso, *Obras*, Clásicos Castellanos, ed. Tomás Navarro Tomás (Madrid, 1911; Internet Archive `obra00vega`). Its introduction STATES the policy: the text follows Herrera's *Anotaciones*, keeps the spellings that have phonetic value (*diño*, *vitoria*), and "se ha modernizado aquello que sólo afecta á la escritura." But its text of the sonnet differs from the 1817 text in words: line 4 reads "enciende el corazón y lo refrena" (the 1817 print has "Con clara luz la tempestad serena"), and it has "airado" and "helado" where 1817 has *ayrado* and *elado*. A change would alter line 4, the notes, the scansion cards, and the exercise items on that poem. For Timothy's decision.
- **San Juan.** The Internet Archive has the Toledo edition of 1912 (`obrasedicincr03john`); no statement on spelling was found in it either.
- **Quevedo.** Menéndez y Pelayo's *Obras completas* (1903, 1907; `obrascompletasd02orbegoog`, `obrascompletasd09pelagoog`) were found; not read for a statement on spelling.
- **Bernal Díaz.** Candidates found: an edition of 1900 (`historia-verdadera-de-la-conquista-de-la-nueva-espana-bernal-diaz-del-castillo`) and the edition of Genaro García that Maudslay translated; not read.
- **Cervantes I.1 and I.8.** Rodríguez Marín's edition (above) contains both; the prologue text agrees in kind with the other passages. A swap would change the card on spelling in IX prose (it discusses *há*, *lantejas*, *viérnes*, *rocin*, which are Aribau's forms) and three of its exercise items.
