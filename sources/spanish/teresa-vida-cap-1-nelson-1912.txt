WORK: Santa Teresa de Jesús (1515-1582), Libro de la vida (written 1562 to 1565; first printed 1588). The author died in 1582.
EDITION OF THE SPANISH: Obras escogidas de la Santa Madre Teresa de Jesús: Libro de su vida; Las Moradas, introducción por Rafael Mesa y López (Londres, París, Edimburgo, Dublín y Nueva York: Thomas Nelson and Sons; the introduction is signed "París, julio de 1912"). The introduction says that the edition does not reproduce the saint's spelling ("no nos ha parecido conveniente reproducir exactamente la ortografía de la santa, pues escribía naide, espiriencia, disposicion, ...") and that the editors have kept "dél" and the contractions. The dates of Mesa y López are not known to the course (B14).

### Spanish (printed page 6)
El tener padres virtuosos y temerosos de Dios me bastara, si yo no fuera tan ruin, con lo que el Señor me favorecía, para ser buena. Era mi padre aficionado á leer buenos libros, y así los tenía de romance para que leyesen sus hijos.

### English (Lewis, section 1)
I had a father and mother, who were devout and feared God. Our Lord also helped me with His grace. All this would have been enough to make me good, if I had not been so wicked. My father was very much given to the reading of good books; and so he had them in Spanish, that his children might read them.
[Matching: the first Spanish sentence is rendered by three English sentences, with the infinitive subject recast as a main verb; the second Spanish sentence by one. The third Spanish sentence ("Estos con el cuidado ..., comenzó á despertarme ...") was not used, because the edition prints the singular comenzó after the plural Estos (De la Fuente's edition, BAE 53, prints the same and notes that other editions read Esto).]
