Real published translation. Lewis died 1897. The print has a note numeral after "heart,"; the course omits it. Differences: Lewis has "hand" for the plural manos, "spear" for dardo (the Academy defines dardo as a thrown weapon like a small, thin lance), "my very entrails" for las entrañas, and he puts "at times" before "into my heart."

### Spanish (printed page 202)
Veíale en las manos un dardo de oro largo, y al fin del hierro me parecía tener un poco de fuego. Este me parecía meter por el corazón algunas veces, y que me llegaba á las entrañas: al sacarle me parecía las llevaba consigo, y me dejaba toda abrasada en amor grande de Dios.

### English (Lewis, section 17)
I saw in his hand a long spear of gold, and at the iron's point there seemed to be a little fire. He appeared to me to be thrusting it at times into my heart, and to pierce my very entrails; when he drew it out, he seemed to draw them out also, and to leave me all on fire with a great love of God.
