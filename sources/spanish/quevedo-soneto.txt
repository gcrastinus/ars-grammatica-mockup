TEXT READ: PDF page 110, printed page 78, right column, poem 281, SONETO XXXI. The heading on the page is "Amor constante más allá de la muerte." The heading is named in the prose of the card and is not inside the verse block. Nothing in the fourteen lines was altered. The footnote under the rule, "(1) La piedra Iman," belongs with the magnet-stone sonnet earlier on the page and is not quoted.

The print writes x in Dexará and dexarán. dexarán is plural. The futures podrá, Dexará, dexarán, tendrá, and serán carry an acute accent, and Médulas carries one. There is no accent on llevare, dia, mia, ardia, fria, afan, prision, or on mas where it means "but."
"Hora ," has a space before the comma. "lisonjera ;" and "ardia ;" and "cuidado ;" have a space before the semicolon. The semicolon after cuidado was read from the ink (a dot above a comma), and the text layer of the page has the same mark.
The mark after sentido descends below the letters and tapers. It is quoted as a comma. The text layer of the same page reads a period there. "enamorado." ends with a period.
"la agua" is kept. The article is la.

### Sonnet (PDF page 110, printed page 78)
Cerrar podrá mis ojos la postrera
Sombra, que me llevare el blanco dia;
Y podrá desatar esta alma mia
Hora , á su afan ansioso lisonjera ;
Mas no de esotra parte en la ribera
Dexará la memoria en donde ardia ;
Nadar sabe mi llama la agua fria,
Y perder el respeto á ley severa;
Alma, á quien todo un Dios prisión ha sido,
Venas, que humor á tanto fuego han dado,
Médulas, que han gloriosamente ardido,
Su cuerpo dexarán, no su cuidado ;
Serán ceniza, mas tendrá sentido,
Polvo serán, mas polvo enamorado.
