### Spanish
Comenzaba este largo Siglo, que ya va de vencida.—No se sabe fijamente el año: sólo consta que era después del de 4 y antes del de 8. Reinaba, pues, todavía en España Don Carlos IV de Borbón; por la gracia de Dios, según las monedas, y por olvido ó gracia especial de Bonaparte, según los boletines franceses.
(In the edition "por la gracia de Dios," is in italics.)

### English (Fassett)
It was toward the beginning of the long century which is now drawing to a close. We do not know the exact year: we can only be certain that it was after 1804, and before 1808. Don Carlos IV still reigned in Spain — by the grace of God, according to the coins, and by the forgetfulness or especial grace of Bonaparte, according to the French bulletins.
(In the English "by the grace of God," is in italics; the first word is printed "IT" in capitals.)
[Matching: three Spanish sentences, three English sentences. The English is free in "It was toward the beginning" (Comenzaba), "1804" and "1808" (del de 4, del de 8), "We do not know" (No se sabe), and it leaves out "pues" and "de Borbón".]
