## Resting on one source, or on an inference

1. The remark that the adjective keeps its written accent in an adverb in *-mente* rests on the Manual's own spelling of the words, not on a stated rule (the rule is the *Ortografía*).
2. The statement that Latin marks a direct object by the accusative alone is general knowledge of the Latin grammar and was not re-read in A&G.
3. The Latin sentence *Pater domī est* is the course's own.
