### Spanish (printed page 220)
CEL.—Refrán viejo es: quien menos procura, alcança mas bien. Pero yo te haré procurando conseguir lo que siendo negligente no haurías. Consuélate, señor, que en vna hora no se ganó Çamora; pero no por esso desconfiaron los combatientes.
[The course quotes the speech without the speaker's name and the dash.]

### English (Mabbe, printed page 118)
CELEST. It is an old Proverbe; He that labours least, often-times gets most. But I will make thee by thy labouring, to obtaine that which by being negligent, thou shouldst never atchieve. For Zamora was not wonne in an houre; yet did not her besiegers for all this despaire. No more was Rome built in one day; nor Troy ruined in a yeere.
[The course quotes through "despaire."]
