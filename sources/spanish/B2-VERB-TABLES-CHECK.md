# Spanish, batch B2: the verb tables of `lang.iii-decl.es/paradigms` checked against the Academy's model tables

Method. The tables on the cards were first read in clean form from Wiktionary (`wiktionary-conjugation-tables-B2.txt`). Every form of the twelve verbs (infinitive, gerund, participle, and the present, preterite, imperfect, future, conditional, present subjunctive, imperfect subjunctive in *-ra* and *-se*, future subjunctive, and the imperatives) was then looked up, as a word, in the text of the Academy's *Manual*, section 4.9 "Modelos de conjugación" (`RAEManualDeLaNuevaGramaticaDeLaLenguaEspanola`, OCR text; 26 Oct 2026). The Academy's models are *amar* (1), *temer* (2), *partir* (3), *decir* (27), *estar* (35), *haber* (36), *hacer* (37), *ir* (38), *poder* (48), *saber* (58), *ser* (61), *tener* (64).

The Academy has no model *hablar*, *comer*, or *vivir*. For these three the stem was replaced by the stem of the Academy's model (*habl-* by *am-*, *com-* by *tem-*, *viv-* by *part-*), and the resulting forms were looked up in the model's table. This confirms the endings, which are all that differ.

Result. All forms were found in the Academy's tables, with these exceptions, none of which is printed in the deck: the form *fueran* (the OCR has damaged that cell), the imperative *ve* (the OCR has lost it), and the infinitive *tener* (the OCR heading of the model). The imperative *ve* is attested in the same section of the Manual (§4.7.2b: "las formas de imperativo ve (tú) y vayan (ustedes)").

Limits. (1) The OCR prints the tables column by column, so the check shows that a form is in the Academy's table block for that model, not that it stands in the right cell. The person and tense of each form were therefore also read against the cells of the Wiktionary table, and the regular forms were derived by rule. (2) For the irregular models that share a page (35 to 38), a form could come from a neighboring model; no irregular form of these verbs is a regular form of another, so this is not a practical risk. (3) The OCR text could not be compared with the printed book.

Further facts used for the cards, with the Manual's section: the *-mos*, *-n*, and second person endings (§4.2.3), the stems of the preterite and future (§4.2.1), *ser* and *ir* share the stem *fu-* in the preterite (§4.7.2a, b), the imperfect of *ser* has the stem *er-* (§4.7.2a), *estoy*, *doy*, *voy*, *soy* (§4.7.1b), the participles *hecho* and *dicho* are irregular (§4.6.2a), the imperative forms *di*, *haz*, *ten*, *ve* (§4.7.1b, §4.7.2b).

The exercise `paradigms-ex` uses verbs that are not printed on the cards (*cantar*, *trabajar*, *beber*, *correr*, *escribir*, *subir*, *estudiar*) and three verbs that the Manual's index lists as conjugated like *tener*: *obtener*, *detener*, and *mantener* (index of verbs, "tener [64]").
