# Spanish, batch B7: fact check of Section V, second half (26 Oct 2026)

Scope: `lang.v.es/mood`, `clauses`, `order`, with their question points and exercises (`mood-ex`, `clause-ex`, `cited-ex`).

Sources: MANUAL is the Academy's *Nueva gramática de la lengua española: Manual* (2010), read as a machine-read text (Internet Archive item RAEManualDeLaNuevaGramaticaDeLaLenguaEspanola; the file `RAE - Manual de la Nueva Gramática de la Lengua Española_djvu.txt`, fetched 26 Oct 2026). A&G is Allen and Greenough's *New Latin Grammar* (1903), read as a machine-read text (Internet Archive item allengreenoughsn01alle; the OCR has lost the macrons of the printed book). The printed books could not be compared (B14). Excerpts: `rae-manual-2010-excerpts-B7.txt`, `allen-greenough-excerpts-B7.txt`. The paragraph numbers are the Manual's own.

How to read the verdicts. "Verified" means that the statement, or the rule that an example applies, is found in the paragraph named. "Course's example" means that the sentence is the course's own, built on a rule that is verified. "Inference" and "one source" mark claims that go into the B14 row of `SPANISH-PLAN.md`. The English remarks that open each card (the uses of "would," "She insists that he be here," and the like) are plain English grammar and are not sourced.
