### Spanish
Era la hora del oscurecer. Las sombras amontonadas en el fondo de la bahía subían ya á lo alto de las montañas. En los pocos buques anclados la tripulación se ocupaba en la carga y descarga, y sus gritos y el chirrido de las maquinillas era lo único que turbaba la paz de aquel recinto.
(In the transcription the passage follows a short dialogue and is divided into paragraphs; the three sentences are the first paragraph of narration of chapter I.)

### English (Dole)
It was the twilight hour: the shadows clustering in the recesses of the bay had already crept far up on the mountains. On the few vessels at anchor the hands were busy loading and unloading their cargoes, and their shouts and the creaking of windlasses were the only sounds that disturbed the peacefulness of the place.
[Matching: three Spanish sentences, two English sentences: Dole joins the first two with a colon. "recinto" is "the place"; the plural "were" for the singular "era".]
