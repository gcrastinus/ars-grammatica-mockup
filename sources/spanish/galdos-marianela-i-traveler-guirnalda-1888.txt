### Spanish
Era un hombre de mediana edad, de complexión recia, de buena talla, ancho de espaldas, resuelto de ademanes, firme de andadura, basto de facciones, de mirar osado y vivo, ligero á pesar de su regular obesidad, y (dígase de una vez, aunque sea prematuro) excelente persona por do quiera que se le mirara.

### English
Our traveller was a man of middle age, strongly built, tall and broad-shouldered; his movements were brisk and resolute, his step firm, his manner somewhat rugged, his eye bold and bright; his pace was nimble, considering that he was decidedly stout, and he was—the reader may at once be told, though somewhat prematurely—as good a soul as you may meet with anywhere.
