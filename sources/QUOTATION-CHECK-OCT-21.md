## Latin

Checked on the Latin Library (Virgil, Cicero, Catullus, Terence, Seneca), Kayser's *Rhetorica ad Herennium* (1854), and the la.wikisource *Appendix Probi*. **Corrected**: *Acamasque Thoasque / Pelidesque Neoptolemus* (*Aeneid* II.262–263), which the language card had given as *… Neoptolemusque*. Verified: *Aeneid* I.179, I.212, I.300–301, I.399, I.573, I.664, III.2–3, IV.93, IV.584–585, IX.427; *Georgics* II.536; *Eclogues* I.69; *In Catilinam* I.1, II.1; Catullus 84.1–2; Terence, *Eunuchus* 732 and *Andria* 218 (the Latin Library prints the contracted *inceptiost*; the course prints *inceptio est*, as Donatus quotes it); Seneca, *Epistulae* 106.12; *Rhetorica ad Herennium* IV.28.39 (words; Kayser's punctuation differs); the five *Appendix Probi* entries. *Aeneid* X.180–181: the editions of Virgil print *Astyr*; the course prints *Astur* as Donatus's example (as the main Section VII card does), and the item now says "as Donatus quotes it." Donatus's own spelling was not checked against Keil or Holtz.
