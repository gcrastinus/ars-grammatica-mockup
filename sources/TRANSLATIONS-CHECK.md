# Translations check (verified 2026-10-16)

Only facts confirmed in library catalogues, publisher pages, or the series' own list are given. Anything not confirmed is marked as such.

## (a) Boethius on the Categories and On Interpretation

### In Peri hermeneias

1. **Ammonius, *On Aristotle's On Interpretation 9*, tr. David Blank, with Boethius, *On Aristotle's On Interpretation 9* (first and second commentaries), tr. Norman Kretzmann**; essays by Richard Sorabji, Norman Kretzmann and Mario Mignucci. Ancient Commentators on Aristotle. London: Duckworth / Ithaca, NY: Cornell University Press, 1998. 216 pp. ISBN 0-8014-3335-5 (Cornell); LCCN 98016299.
   - **Covers:** Boethius's comments on chapter 9 only (future contingents / the sea battle), from **both** the first and second editions. Boethius part is translated by **Kretzmann**, not Blank.
   - Sources: ECU library catalogue record (https://librarycatalog.ecu.edu/catalog/645876); ACA list (https://www.ancientcommentators.org.uk/translations-by-publication-date.html, no. 29).
   - Related, but not Boethius: Ammonius, *On Aristotle On Interpretation 1–8*, tr. David L. Blank (London: Duckworth, 1996; ISBN 0-7156-2657-4).
2. **Boethius, *On Aristotle On Interpretation 1–3*, tr. Andrew Smith.** Ancient Commentators on Aristotle. London: Duckworth, 2010. viii + 166 pp. ISBN 0-7156-3918-8. Reissued Bloomsbury Academic, 2014 (ISBN 978-1-4725-0032-8).
   - **Covers:** Books 1–3 of the **second** commentary (*editio secunda*), i.e. Aristotle *De int.* chs. 1 to roughly 3.
   - Source: UC Berkeley catalogue; ACA list no. 81; historyoflogic.com Boethius bibliography (contents: Book 1 p.15, Book 2 p.57, Book 3 p.115).
3. **Boethius, *On Aristotle On Interpretation 4–6*, tr. Andrew Smith.** Ancient Commentators on Aristotle. London: Bristol Classical Press, 2011. viii + 151 pp. ISBN 978-0-7156-3919-1. Reissued Bloomsbury Academic, 2014 (ISBN 978-1-4725-0165-3).
   - **Covers:** Books 4–6 of the **second** commentary. (Book 3 ends before ch. 9, which is covered by Kretzmann 1998.)
   - Sources: Classical Review 63.1 (2013) 295–7 review; Bloomsbury product page; ACA list no. 82.
   - Together with Kretzmann 1998, these give most of the second commentary in English. I found **no complete English translation of the first commentary (*editio prima*)**; only its ch. 9 portion (Kretzmann 1998) was verified.

### In Categorias

- **No published English translation of Boethius's *In Categorias Aristotelis* was found** in the ACA series list or in other searches. The Latin text is PL 64, 159A–294C (online at DigilibLT and P. King's Toronto site). Only Boethius's Latin *translation* of the Categories is online with a facing English Aristotle (Logic Museum), and that is not a translation of the commentary. Treat this as "not found", not as proven absent.

### Kielhorn and other Paspaśā translations

- **Kielhorn: no English translation of the Paspaśāhnika by him was verified.** What he did publish in English in 1876: "On the Mahâbhâshya", *Indian Antiquary* 5 (1876) 241–251 (public-domain PDF, Heidelberg repository: https://fid4sa-repository.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/4766/), and *Kâtyâyana and Patañjali: Their Relation to Each Other and to Pâṇini* (Bombay, 1876; 64 pp.). These are studies, not translations. His major work is the critical **edition** *The Vyākaraṇa-Mahābhāṣya of Patañjali*, 3 vols. (Bombay 1880–85; 3rd ed. rev. K. V. Abhyankar, Poona: BORI, 1962–72).
- **J. R. Ballantyne (Mirzapore, 1856)** printed the Sanskrit text of the opening Navāhnika with Kaiyaṭa and Nāgeśa. It is an **edition, not a translation** (EAP/British Library; Karttunen).
- Joshi & Roodbergen 1986 (above) is the only English translation of the Paspaśāhnika I could verify. If other translations are cited (e.g. older Indian-published renderings), they still need checking.
